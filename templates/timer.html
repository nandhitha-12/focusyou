🧑‍🏫 Feynman Technique
Named after Nobel physicist Richard Feynman: "If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it yet."
1
Choose — Write the concept name at the top of a blank page
2
Explain Simply — Teach it as if to a 10-year-old. No jargon!
3
Find Gaps — Where did you get stuck? Go back to notes
4
Simplify — Use real-life analogies to solidify it
⏱️ Suggested Time Per Step
Step 1–2: 20 min — Write your explanation
Step 3: 10 min — Review & find gaps
Step 4: 10 min — Refine with analogies

📖 How 52/17 Works
1
Work with 100% focus for 52 minutes — single task only
2
Take a true 17-min break — walk, stretch, no scrolling!
3
Repeat cycles — most productive workers do 3–4 per day
4
Based on Draugiem Group research on top 10% productive workers 📊
🆚 52/17 vs Pomodoro
🍅Pomodoro — 25/5 min. Great for beginners & varied tasks.
⚡52/17 — 52/17 min. Better for deep work & complex problems.
